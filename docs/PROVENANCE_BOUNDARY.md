# GLUBALL provenance boundary

GLUBALL begins from an independently written geometry contract and implementation.

Historical VORTEX, VORTEX2, NEXUS, and archived DOI materials were supplied privately as migration/audit references so that the replacement boundary could be identified. Their previous mouth/centre, gate/centre/mouth, D1/D2, mouth/anus, centre-transfer, and related presentation geometry are **not** part of `GLUBALL-KNOT-V1` and are not used by `gluball-core.js`.

The current implementation is derived from the explicit `(2,3)` torus-knot and tube equations in `docs/GLUBALL_KNOT.md`. The renderer is new code written against that contract.

General engineering ideas that may later be reintroduced—bounded logical sampling, exact integer index mapping, deterministic batching, ternary/triality metadata, receipts, Fibonacci/phi sampling, sonification, capture/export, and accelerator residual testing—are treated as separable software techniques. Any such capability must be adapted to GLUBALL's geometry rather than restoring the removed centre-transfer semantics.

This document records a technical provenance boundary. It is not a legal determination about ownership of historical repositories or releases.
