THE SHAPE OF THE EXPERIMENT

One closed curve.
Plenty to get tangled in.

The tube follows an independently specified (2,3) torus knot. Its amber host-torus guide makes the two winding directions visible. Camera, lighting and overlays are presentation settings; the geometry and exact sampling remain fixed.

Integer ticks and SHA-256 receipts make state reproducible. This is a geometry study, not a QCD simulation or a computed knot fibration. Tube embeddedness is not yet a machine-checked theorem.

References & contracts

Visual inspiration: Jesse Bettencourt’s Torus Knot Fibration. Its Seifert surfaces and stereographic construction are separate mathematics; this viewer implements surface and wire views of GLUBALL only.
